A Post Mortem

Theme

August 13th 2026 #js13k theme was announced to much anticipation.

What would follow Black Cat, Triskaidekaphobia, 13th Century and Death?

Errmm... Rainbows and Unicorns. 🤷 Ok, let's get to it!

Ideas

Recently, I've been marvelling at the UF0 50 collection. Boasting an impressive range of games that, at first, seem simple only to reveal hidden depths after a few plays. A masterclass in game design. Magic Garden, in particular, drew me in with it's quirky blend of snake and pacman.

Another source of inspiration has been KOLYDR. Beautifully simplistic and super addictive. A delicous balance of risk and reward.

Engine

Following on last year's effort I reached for the tried and tested LittleJS. The more I use it the more I appreciate just how great it is; comprehensive documentation, buttery smooth performance and a very well thought out API. Among other cool things particles, tilemaps, webgl, vectors are all included.

The crunch

This year problems were of a time nature rather than technical. August had long slipped out the backdoor and September was threatening to go the same way. By the time 9th Sept rolled around, I hadn't yet started. Oops! Well, what's better than a tight deadline to get things done?

With some vague idea of a Magic Garden / KOLYDR riff, I pushed some pixels:

Early attempts revolved around the player tapping / clicking to move the player on screen. This nicely sidesteps any need for clunky mobile controls. Unfortunately, in practice it just got too confusing as the screen filled up and a hovering finger covered vital elements.

Replacing this with basic WASD / cursors or swipe on mobile improved the playing experience. As an extra bonus, LittleJS provides solid joypad support out of the box.

The LLM in the room

The development landscape has changed seismically in the past couple of years. It can no longer be disputed that AI turbo charges development. This often comes at a cost, though. Particularly when vibecoding; quick inital results leading to a tangled mess of spaghetti.

Wary, of this I just banged out caffeine fuelled lines of code. As the deadline loomed and the byte budget receeded I did run some classes through gemini and claude to look for optimizations. Undoubtedly, this saved some space but allowed me to retain a mental map of the code which is a massive benefit for making quick changes and tweaks.

Wrapping up

As always, the js13k jam is awesome; I love the constraints and seeing what great games other participants cook up.

My only reget, this year, is not spending more time on my entry. While not unhappy with Glowbugs I think some more variety and tweaks would have vastly improved the game. Ah well, there is always next year!

If you've got this far, thanks and what are you waiting for? Go play the game
