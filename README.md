## About

Retro style arcade game, featuring rainbows,
unicorns and poop. Lots of poop.

Dark forces have sucked the colors from our world.
Everything is dull and gray.
You, Uma the Unicorn, must save the day.
Collect glowbugs to re-ignite the rainbow.

## How To Play

Swipe / Arrow keys / WASD / Gamepad

- Eat cupcakes
- Cupcake turns to Magic Poop
- Magic Poop turns into Glowbug
- Collect enough Glowbugs and escape
- Avoid nasties
